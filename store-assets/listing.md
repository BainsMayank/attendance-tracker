# Google Play listing — English

## Title

BunkBuddy: Attendance Tracker

## Short description

Track subject attendance, organize your timetable and stay above your target

## Full description

BunkBuddy helps students keep attendance in perspective. See where you stand in every subject, keep your timetable organized and build a clear daily record throughout the semester.

KNOW WHERE YOU STAND
See overall and per-subject attendance percentages alongside attended, missed and total classes. Set a semester attendance target, such as 75%, or give a subject its own target. Clear status labels highlight subjects that are safe, near the line or below the minimum.

PLAN AROUND YOUR TARGET
Understand your attendance buffer and how many upcoming classes you need to attend to reach your target. Explore attendance scenarios before making plans. Calculations use the records and class weights you enter; keep them up to date and follow your institution's attendance rules.

KEEP A DAILY RECORD
Mark classes present, absent or neutral, or mark all classes present together. Neutral classes do not count toward your percentage. Add class notes, record extra classes and manage holidays without losing your attendance history.

PUT YOUR TIMETABLE IN ORDER
Build a weekly timetable with subject names, class types, times and rooms. See free gaps, handle alternating weeks and update your schedule as the semester changes. You can also import timetable JSON with a preview and conflict checks. Optional photo-to-timetable help uses an external service that you choose; the app does not read photos with built-in AI.

SEE THE SEMESTER CLEARLY
Review attendance history, trends and a subject breakdown. Keep archived semesters available for reference and start a new semester when you are ready.

KEEP STUDY MATERIALS TOGETHER
Organize units and topics, save notes and files, add deadlines and links, and use a focus timer within a subject.

YOUR WORKSPACE, ON YOUR PHONE
Core attendance tracking, timetable, notes and history work offline without an account. Create local backups, export JSON or ZIP backups and preview a backup before restoring it. Backups are not encrypted; save exported copies somewhere you trust. External links, external timetable helpers and cloud destinations selected in your phone's share sheet may require internet access.

Make attendance a habit, spot subjects that need attention and plan your semester with a clearer view of your progress.
